THE EXTRACTED GATES vs THE RECOVERED RTL
======================================================================

Two independent descriptions, two independent simulators, one
vector set: the unique solution, an empty grid, a full grid,
one-star near misses, random sparse grids, two-per-row grids,
two-per-row-and-column permutation pairs, and 24 grids that get
every count right and only break the no-touch rule. That last
class is what distinguishes a four-message reading of the output
generator from the five-message one. Every cycle of success and
of O[7:0] is compared.

EQUIVALENCE: 0 success mismatches, 0 O mismatches over 564 grids
RESULT: the recovered RTL is cycle-equivalent to the extracted netlist
